PRIVACY POLICY

How Grillista & RK Group of Industries collect, protect, and handle applicant and customer information.

● 100% Secure Data Protection Guaranteed

1. Information We Collect

When you submit a franchise inquiry, download an investor prospectus PDF, or interact with our platforms, Grillista / RK Group of Industries may collect personal information that you voluntarily provide, including:

  • Contact details: Full Name, Phone Number, WhatsApp Number, Email Address.
  • Franchise criteria: Proposed City/State, Property Ownership Status, Space Size (sq.ft.), Budget Tier.
  • Technical data: IP address, device type, browser information for analytical security.

2. How We Use Your Information

We utilize collected personal data strictly for corporate franchise business operations:

  • Evaluating franchise applicant eligibility and territory availability in your requested city.
  • Connecting you directly with our Corporate Franchise Development Managers (+91 63868 18682).
  • Sending requested investment prospectus kits, FOFO P&L models, and franchise details.
  • Providing 24/7 support via our Grillista AI Assistant and WhatsApp hotline.

3. Data Confidentiality & Non-Disclosure

Your personal information is handled with strict confidentiality. We DO NOT sell, rent, trade, or leak applicant data to third-party telemarketers or external agencies. Information is accessed only by authorized RK Group of Industries franchise managers for store setup evaluation.

4. Cookies & Web Analytics

Our website uses session storage and essential cookies to remember your preferences (such as light/dark mode settings, applicant submission status, and calculator inputs). You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though certain interactive features may function with limited capabilities.

5. Your Data Rights & Deletion Requests

You have the right to request access to your submitted data, request corrections, or ask us to permanently delete your franchise inquiry record from our corporate system anytime. To exercise your rights, email our team at grillistakanpur@gmail.com or call +91 63868 18682.
