1

Scope of Information We Collect

When you submit a franchise application, request financial details, or use our interactive territory tools, Grillista / RK Group of Industries collects information you voluntarily provide:

Personal Contact Data Full Name, Mobile Number, WhatsApp Contact, Email Address.
Franchise Profile Proposed City, Property Ownership, Investment Budget Tier.
2

How We Use Your Information

Your data is processed strictly for legitimate corporate expansion and franchise onboarding purposes:

  • Evaluating prospective franchise partners and city territory eligibility.
  • Connecting you directly with corporate franchise relationship managers at RK Group.
  • Sending requested Franchise Prospectus PDFs and official P&L documentation.
  • Maintaining secure communication logs for corporate compliance.
3

Data Security & Storage Architecture

We employ enterprise-grade security architecture to safeguard your personal details against unauthorized access or disclosure:

100% Confidentiality & Encryption:
All online form submissions are encrypted via SSL/TLS protocols. Franchise inquiry records are stored in secure corporate cloud infrastructure managed by RK Group of Industries.
4

Third-Party Non-Disclosure Rule

We NEVER sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal information to third-party marketing companies.

Information is accessed strictly by authorized corporate officers of Grillista and RK Group of Industries, or shared only when required by applicable Indian law enforcement agencies under valid legal orders.

5

Cookies & Tracking Technology Standard

Our website uses basic session cookies to enhance browsing speed, remember your city search preferences, and analyze anonymized site traffic metrics.

You may choose to disable cookies in your web browser settings at any time without affecting your ability to view franchise information.

6

Your Applicant Rights & Data Deletion Control

As an applicant or visitor, you retain full rights over your personal data:

  • Request a copy of personal information submitted to Grillista.
  • Request correction or updating of contact details.
  • Request permanent deletion of your franchise application record from our corporate database.
7

Policy Modifications & Amendments

Grillista / RK Group reserves the right to update or amend this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes in Indian privacy legislation or corporate procedures.

All updates will be published on this page with an updated "Effective Date" at the top of the policy.
