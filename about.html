About Grillista

THE ULTIMATE FOOD CHAIN • Fast-growing 100% pure vegetarian café brand offering hygienic, affordable, and delicious food with a modern café experience across India.

Incubated & Operated Brand

Backed by Corporate Systems & Long-Term Vision

Grillista is not an experimental food startup — it is a well-executed, taste-consistent food brand incubated, funded, and operated under RK Group of Industries (www.shreerkgroup.com).

With 3 successfully running operational outlets in Kanpur and a strong online delivery presence (Swiggy & Zomato), Grillista provides a structured, low-risk, and highly profitable franchise model across Pan-India (Tier-1, Tier-2, Tier-3 cities).
