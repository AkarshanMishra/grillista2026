About Us

The Story of Veg Vibes & Flame-Grilled Excellence

Born in Kanpur, Grillista is India's premier 100% pure veg fast-casual franchise redefining gourmet flame-grilled dining.

Our Journey from Kanpur to Pan-India

Grillista started with a simple yet powerful mission: to bring authentic charcoal flame-grilled flavor to 100% vegetarian fast-casual dining. What began as our flagship outlet in Barra, Kanpur quickly expanded into Kakadeo after overwhelming love from students, families, and food lovers.

Today, Grillista stands as "THE ULTIMATE FOOD CHAIN", combining secret tandoori marinades, automated chef-less kitchens, and positive energy store ambiance engineered for maximum franchisee profitability.
