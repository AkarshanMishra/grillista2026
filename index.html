FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

QUESTIONS YOU MIGHT ASK

Where are Grillista's live operational outlets located?

Grillista operates 2 flagship outlets in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh under RK Group of Industries:
  • Outlet 1 (Barra 2): Main Bypass Road, Barra 2, Kanpur (Dine-In & Drive-Thru).
  • Outlet 2 (Kakadeo): Coaching Hub Market, Kakadeo, Kanpur (Express Hub).

Is Grillista 100% Vegetarian and certified pure?

Yes! Grillista is 100% Pure Veg. We maintain zero cross-contamination risk, serving gourmet charcoal paneer tikka burgers, tandoori soya wraps, charred bites, and positive energy shakes.

What franchise models are available?

We offer two turnkey investment formats in Indian Rupees:
  • Mini Bistro Model (600+ sq ft): ₹12 Lakh total investment *(Machinery ₹5.5L, Raw Material ₹2.5L, Fee ₹3L, Branding ₹1L)*.
  • Classic Lounge Model (700-1000+ sq ft): ₹16 Lakh total investment *(Machinery ₹7.5L, Raw Material ₹3L, Fee ₹4.5L, Branding ₹1L)*.

What is included in the Turnkey Store Setup?

Turnkey setup includes kitchen machinery & equipment, initial raw material inventory stock, 3D store interior blueprinting, staff training, POS software, and grand opening marketing support.

What site location parameters do you require?

Stores should be located in high footfall commercial zones, student hub markets, drive-thrus, or high-street markets with minimum 15-20 ft frontage and 600+ sq ft floor area.

Which cities are open for franchise booking?

Territories in Kanpur (Swaroop Nagar, Kidwai Nagar, Chakeri), Lucknow, Noida, Greater Noida, Varanasi, Prayagraj, and major Tier-2 cities in UP & India are currently open for booking.

How does the 100% Chef-Less Kitchen Model work?

Our central kitchen supplies standardized pre-portioned marinades, sauces, and patties. Your staff receives 10 days of hands-on training at our Kakadeo flagship outlet so anyone can assemble orders in 90 seconds without a skilled chef.

What marketing and online food delivery support is provided?

Grillista HQ manages grand opening influencer PR blasts, local Google My Business SEO, social media ads, and direct corporate onboarding on Swiggy and Zomato for day-one online orders.

What are the net profit margins and ROI timeline?

Our operating outlets achieve an average 35% Net Profit Margin (FOFO Model) and 65% gross margins. Estimated payback period for initial investment is typically 12 to 18 months based on location footfall.

What are royalty fees and contract tenure?

Royalty is 5% of monthly gross sales (starts ONLY after 6 months of store operation). Monthly maintenance fee is ₹5,000. Franchise agreement tenure is 5 Years (Renewable).
