Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Grillista franchise investment models, Kanpur outlets, and operating margins.

Grillista currently operates flagship outlets in Kanpur, Uttar Pradesh:
  • Outlet 1 (Barra): Barra 2, Main Bypass Road, Kanpur (Dine-In & Drive-Thru).
  • Outlet 2 (Kakadeo): Coaching Hub Market, Kakadeo, Kanpur (Express Hub).
  • Outlet 3 (Juhi Saket Nagar): VR Tower, Saket Nagar, Kanpur (Rooftop Celebration Outlet).
We offer two transparent investment formats in Indian Rupees:
  • Mini Bistro Model (600+ sq ft): ₹12 Lakh Total Investment *(Machinery ₹5.5L, Raw Material ₹2.5L, Franchise Fee ₹3L, Branding & Layout ₹1L)*.
  • Classic Lounge Model (700-1000+ sq ft): ₹16 Lakh Total Investment *(Machinery ₹7.5L, Raw Material ₹3L, Franchise Fee ₹4.5L, Branding & Layout ₹1L)*.
*Interiors excluded.
Royalty is set at 5% of gross monthly sales, which starts only after 6 months of store operation to maximize early franchisee cashflow. Maintenance fee is ₹5,000 / month and agreement period is 5 Years.
No prior restaurant experience is required. Grillista uses a 100% chef-less kitchen model with standardized recipes and central supply delivery. We provide 10 days of hands-on training at our Kakadeo outlet for your kitchen staff and store manager.
